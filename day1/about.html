About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to capture your immediate thoughts and daily tasks. It provides a simple, structured interface for organizing ideas without unnecessary friction.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought or reminder into the note input field.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to add it to your list.
  3. Review and manage your saved notes under the active notes section.

Features

  • Fast, client-side note drafting.
  • Accessible semantic layout following HTML5 standards.
  • Built-in keyboard accessibility and screen reader support.
